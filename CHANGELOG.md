# 5.8.0

This one's mostly about looks. Most of Blizzard's windows got redone, you get your own buff and debuff frames, and the character sheet picked up a few handy tricks.

**Blizzard windows**

Most of the Blizzard windows BluUI skins have a new, cleaner look. The auction house, group finder, transmog, guild and spellbook are the big ones, but smaller windows like the trainer, the calendar and the Barber Shop got it too. Anything redone is tagged NEW LOOK on the skins page.

The skins stay in calm greys now and only show your accent colour when you hover something. If you'd rather have different colours, the Theme page has a Blizzard windows section for them.

**Game menu and quests**

The game menu has been redesigned. The objective tracker is redone too, and the quest log next to the world map shows your quests as cards.

**Buffs and debuffs**

BluUI has its own buff and debuff frames now, and they're on by default under Buffs & Debuffs. You can sort them by time or by name and move the timer and stack count around. There's a blacklist for anything you never want to see. They replace the old Player Auras skin.

**Character sheet**

Empty gem sockets show on the character sheet and when you inspect someone. Click one to socket a gem straight from your bags. That replaces the Gem Manager, which is gone now.

Tier pieces are marked in green and embellished ones in gold, and enchants show their full name. The arrows for swapping gear are now a small tab beside each slot. It only lists gear you can actually wear, and it greys out when there's nothing to swap.

You can open the character sheet in combat now, and the inspect window doesn't land on top of it anymore.

**Keystone card**

The keystone card shows for groups you list yourself now, and it stays put while the group fills up. Joining someone else's key doesn't make it vanish either. Once a key is done, the card pops up again while you're still inside, so you can port straight to the next one.

**Group finder**

Full groups are greyed out, and hovering one tells you why. The roles you pick get a coloured band behind them so it's easy to see what you're queued as.

**Pet battles**

The datatext bars, objective tracker and stats readout hide during pet battles, and the pet battle screen has the new look.

**Menus**

Dropdown menus are solid now. They highlight what you hover and close when you scroll the page. The talent loadout, loot spec and minimap tracking menus match the rest.

**Other bits**

Boss frames show target and mouseover borders. Shields show on your target even at full health. Item names in chat aren't cut short anymore, and BattleTags show in Battle.net blue. Raid boss confetti is off by default now, so turn it back on if you miss it.

There's a handful of fixes too, like the error when opening the world map, a scroll bar error in combat, and the auction house forgetting Current Expansion Only.

If the Cooldown Manager ever feels slow, type /bui cdm, play for a while, then type it again. You'll get a report you can copy into a bug report.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
