# 5.14.0

You can list your own group and sort through applicants in the new Premade Groups window now.

**Premade Groups**

Start a group right from the window. Pick your dungeon from the pictures, and your keystone is already picked for you. Competitive is the default playstyle, and a preview shows how your group will look in the list.

Once you're listed, the applicants window takes over. Each applicant shows their spec, item level, rating, best run in your dungeon and their server. You can filter them by role, physical or caster damage, lust, rating, item level, region and server. Right-click someone to whisper them or hide their server.

It isn't just for Mythic+. Raids, delves and custom groups work too.

When you're looking for a group, there are new filters for groups that have lust and for physical or caster comps. The search bar sits in the toolbar with a proper blinking cursor, and the refresh button is right next to it.

The window stays where you put it when you switch between searching, listing and applicants.

It's still off by default. Turn it on from the Skins page if you want to try it.

**Keystone reminder**

The portal reminder shows up when you build the group yourself, not just when you join one.

**Smart Misdirection**

The Misdirect macro follows whatever Misdirection is for your current spec instead of a fixed name.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
