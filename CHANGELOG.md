# 5.8.4

A quick fix for the death recap window.

**Death recap**

The Close button at the bottom of the death recap had an X drawn right over its text. It's a normal button again, and the X stays in the corner where it belongs.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
