# 5.13.0

A durability warning, every lust in one icon, and a pile of fixes from your reports.

**Weaker Auras**

There's a durability warning now. Pick the percentage it should warn at, and it shows while you drag the slider so you can see where it sits.

The Bloodlust tracker has an icon look that tracks every lust, Heroism, Time Warp and the rest, in a single icon.

The General tab has an eye at the top that previews every weaker aura at once, handy when you're lining things up.

**Keystone reminder**

It shows the leader's key level now, and it stops showing your old listing when you join a new group.

**Cooldown Manager**

Buffs you untick stay hidden after a reload. Custom bars show greyed out when CDM is off instead of vanishing from the settings.

**Menus**

Right-click menus don't open as an empty box anymore after another addon used a dropdown. Checkmarks in BluUI's own menus stay right when a menu redraws itself.

**Premade Groups**

There's an early version of a new Premade Groups window for Mythic+. It's off for now while we finish it. If you want to try it, turn it on from the Skins page.

**Fixes**

The minimap datatext bar hides when the minimap module is off. Alert position options grey out when they don't apply to how the alert is anchored. Group finder status labels don't float over other windows. The ready check and Battle.net toast skins don't throw errors when you turn them off. Trinket icons don't error on items without a cooldown, and PvP talent icons in the inspect window look right again.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
