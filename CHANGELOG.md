# 5.4.0

Setup got a proper overhaul, plus a few fixes.

**Setup**

Setup now starts by asking which profile you want. Pick Blu and you get Bluhu's setup exactly as it's played, and it skips straight to the end. Keep your own profile or make a new one, and it walks you through UI scale, skins, unit frames and action bars.

If you run Bartender4, Dominos or ElvUI's action bars, setup spots them and asks which bars you want. Keep theirs, or use ours and BluUI turns the other addon off on that character.

The last page shows everything you picked, and you can click any of it to go back and change it. Yes, there's confetti. Setup also dims the rest of your screen now, the Back and Next buttons don't jump around anymore, and BluUI's skins are on by default.

**Fixes**

The Power page no longer throws an error for Shadow Priests, Elemental Shamans, Balance Druids and Devourer Demon Hunters who show their mana or soul fragments as text only.

Match anchor width is back for cast bars.

All on and All off on the Skins and Modules pages now light up the one that's actually true.

**Look**

The Great Vault card on the dashboard is easier to read. The text is white and the reward art isn't washed out anymore.

The minimap's Tracking & Tools menu, the Friends and Guild popups and the recent pulls list on the combat datatext now match the rest of BluUI.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
