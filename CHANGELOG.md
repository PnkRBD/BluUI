# 5.8.2

A quick fix for the leave vehicle button.

**Leave vehicle**

Clicking the X to leave a vehicle didn't do anything, so you were stuck using the game's own exit. It works again, including in combat and for quests where you take control of something, like the Loa avatars in Zul'Aman.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
