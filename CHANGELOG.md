# 5.8.3

Ready for patch 12.1.5, with better buffs and debuffs on your party and raid frames.

**Party and raid auras**

The Boss & Mob debuff rule is now Boss & Role Auras. It also shows the debuffs Blizzard flags for your role, like tank swap debuffs, the same way Blizzard's own raid frames do. If you were using it, you get this automatically.

There are a few new rules to pick from too. Priority Debuffs shows the ones Blizzard's raid frames always put first, and party frames now show them by default. Stealable Buffs and Buffs I Can Cast are there for buff rows.

You can pin buffs on your party and raid frames now, under Filters, and switch on "Only show pinned buffs" if that's all you want to see. There's no debuff list, because Blizzard doesn't let addons pick out debuffs on your group by spell. The unit frame pin settings explain that limit more clearly now too.

**Summon icon**

Party and raid frames show when someone is being summoned, and whether they accepted or declined. You can move it, resize it or turn it off with the other indicators.

**Fixes**

The accepted side of the trade window is green again instead of white. Unlocking your action bars doesn't pull the experience bar on screen anymore. There's a handful of small fixes too, like an error when right-clicking unit frames, and one when opening settings for the first time in a session.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
