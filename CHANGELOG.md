# 5.16.1

A quick fix for the keystone reminder.

**Keystone reminder**

The key at the top now says whose it is, with their role and class colour, instead of showing your own role. The party leader's key leads when they have one, and the owner isn't repeated in the rows below. The rows also stop throwing a protected function error when the reminder repaints during combat.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
