# 5.4.0

Setup got a proper overhaul this time, plus a few fixes and some polish.

**Setup**

Setup now starts by asking which profile you want. Pick Blu to get Bluhu's setup as it is, and it takes you straight to the end. Keep the profile you're on or make your own, and it walks you through scale, skins, unit frames and action bars.

The new action bars step looks for other bar addons like Bartender4 or Dominos. If it finds one, you choose. Keep theirs, or use ours and BluUI turns the other one off for that character.

The last page shows what you picked on cards you can click to go back and change something. There's confetti. Setup also dims the rest of the screen now, Back and Next stay in the same spot on every page, and BluUI's skins are picked by default.

**Fixes**

The Power page no longer throws an error on Shadow Priest, Elemental Shaman, Balance Druid and Devourer Demon Hunter when the extra resource is shown as text.

Match anchor width is back on the cast bars.

All on and All off on the Skins and Modules pages now light up to match what's actually switched on.

**Look**

The Great Vault card on the dashboard is easier to read, with white text and brighter slot art.

The minimap's Tracking & Tools menu and the Friends, Guild and Recent pulls panels match the rest of BluUI now.

Updating by hand: delete the old BluUI and BluUI_Options folders, then extract the new ones. Your settings are kept. Restart the game rather than /reload.
